Privacy policy

Last updated October 25, 2026

Prep reviews (prep.prabhavjalan.com) is a free student project run by Prabhav Jalan. It tells you which NeetCode problems to redo each day and can email you a reminder each morning. This page explains what it stores about you, why, and how to have it deleted.

What it stores

  • From your Google account, when you sign in: your name, your email address and the ID Google gives your account. The app never sees your Google password and asks Google for nothing else, such as your contacts, files or calendar.
  • Your time zone, detected by your browser and editable in Settings, so that "today" and the morning email follow your own clock.
  • What you log: which problems you attempted, when, and how it went (Easy, Struggled or Failed), plus the review schedule worked out from that.
  • Whether morning emails are on, and a record of each one sent: the day, how many problems it listed, and the email service's message ID.

How it's used

  • To sign you in, show your reviews, and send the morning email, which you can turn off in Settings.
  • To report how the project went: totals such as the number of users and reviews completed may be published, never names or email addresses.
  • It is never sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone except the services below.

Cookies

One cookie keeps you signed in. It holds only your account number, is signed so it can't be altered, and lasts 30 days. There are no analytics or advertising cookies.

Services that handle your data

  • Render (render.com) runs the app and its database in the United States. Its request logs include IP addresses and are kept for a limited time.
  • Resend (resend.com) delivers the morning emails, so it receives your email address and the email's contents.
  • Google handles sign-in.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data is kept while your account exists. To have your account and everything logged with it deleted, email prabhavjalan12@gmail.com from the address you sign in with. If the app shuts down, its database is deleted.

Questions

Email prabhavjalan12@gmail.com. If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date.
